## Open questions
| ID | Question | Why it matters | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-001 | What are the rules, costs, and limits of magic in each era? | Defines what the protagonist can reproduce or improve. | Establish the core magic system and distinguish practical engineering from new theory. |
| Q-002 | What makes the future dystopian, and how did its society become that way? | Defines the contrast between the protagonist's original era and the past. | Identify the dystopia's features without presupposing a cause. |
| Q-003 | What causes the protagonist's death and displacement into the past, and are they connected? | May define the central mystery. | Decide what he knows and what remains unknown. |
| Q-004 | What is the new continent like, and who already lives there? | Determines frontier opportunities, conflicts, and moral stakes. | Develop geography and inhabitants without assuming the continent is empty. |
| Q-005 | What does the host's family and household expect from him, given his reputation? | Establishes immediate social pressure. | Build key relationships and obligations. |
| Q-006 | What are the protagonist's goals and the novel's central conflict? | Focuses worldbuilding on the story. | Decide after clarifying the protagonist's goals. |
| Q-007 | What does magical industrialization consist of, and what stage has the earlier era reached? | Makes the technological gap concrete. | Define representative differences in energy, production, transport, communication, tools, and institutions. |
| Q-008 | What authority, resources, obligations, and people come with the son's barony? | Establishes the immediate frontier situation. | Define the grant's practical scope. |
| Q-009 | How do the protagonist's two sets of memories coexist in his mind? | Central to his identity and everyday experience in the host's body. | Define the felt strangeness and emotional experience without assuming a specific answer. |
| Q-009 | How do the protagonist's two sets of memories coexist in his mind? | This is central to his identity and day-to-day experience in the host's body. | Define the felt strangeness, memory access, emotional associations, and whether either life feels more personally immediate without assuming a particular answer. |
