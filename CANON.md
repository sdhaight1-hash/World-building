## Proposed ideas
| ID | Idea | Potential benefit | Risks / questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROP-001 | The host's disreputable reputation may give the protagonist freedom to behave differently without immediate scrutiny. | Provides social cover. | Depends on who knows the host and how they react. |
| PROP-002 | The protagonist may apply advanced magical knowledge to accelerate developments in the past. | Creates a progression engine. | Must account for missing materials, infrastructure, institutions, and limits of his knowledge. |

## Open questions
| ID | Question | Why it matters | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-001 | What are the rules, costs, and limits of magic in each era? | Defines what the protagonist can reproduce or improve. | Establish the core magic system and distinguish practical engineering from new theory. |
| Q-002 | What is the world-ending magical catastrophe, and what causes it? | Defines the threat that killed the protagonist's civilization. | Determine observable effects before deciding the ultimate cause. |
| Q-003 | Is the catastrophe connected to his death and displacement into the past? | Could be central to the mystery. | Keep undecided until deliberately established. |
| Q-004 | Was his death caused directly by the catastrophe, and was the displacement accidental or deliberate? | May define the central mystery. | Decide what he knows and what remains unknown. |
| Q-005 | Why was the duke granted the barony, and who understands it as exile? | Shapes family politics. | Define the duke's political circumstances. |
| Q-006 | What is the new continent like, and who already lives there? | Determines frontier opportunities, conflicts, and moral stakes. | Develop geography and inhabitants without assuming the continent is empty. |
| Q-007 | What does the host's family and household expect from him? | Establishes immediate social pressure. | Build key relationships and obligations. |
| Q-008 | What tone and central conflict should guide the novel? | Focuses worldbuilding on the story. | Decide after clarifying the protagonist's goals. |
| Q-009 | What does magical industrialization consist of, and what stage has the earlier era reached? | Makes the technological gap concrete. | Define representative differences in energy, production, transport, communication, tools, and institutions. |
