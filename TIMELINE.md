## Timeline checks
- Does each event have a plausible cause?
- Could the people, information, and resources involved actually reach the relevant place in time?
- Which events are documented history, contested history, myth, or deliberate misinformation?
- Do character ages and travel durations agree with this chronology?
- Do not imply that the catastrophe caused the time displacement unless that connection is deliberately established.
