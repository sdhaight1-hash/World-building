## Core premise

A magical researcher in his twenties dies in a highly advanced magical society. His soul is transported nearly one thousand years into the past and inhabits the body of a recently deceased eighteen-year-old low-ranking noble.

The body belonged to the son of a duke. The young man had a reputation as a wastrel and died in his sleep after drinking too much alcohol. His father, the duke, had recently been granted a small barony on a newly discovered continent across the Vast Sea from the Empire. The grant is effectively an exile to the frontier.

The protagonist must navigate an earlier era while carrying knowledge and experience from a much more magically advanced society.

## Foundational truths
1. The protagonist originally lived in a highly advanced magical society and worked as a magical researcher.
2. After his death, his soul travels nearly one thousand years into the past and inhabits the body of an eighteen-year-old who has recently died.
3. The host body belonged to a duke's son whose father was granted a small barony on a newly discovered continent across the Vast Sea from the Empire; the grant is effectively frontier exile.
4. The host was known as a wastrel and died in his sleep after excessive alcohol consumption.
