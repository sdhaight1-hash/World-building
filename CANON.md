# Canon and Open Questions

Use this file as the authority index. Detailed entries should live in their own files and link back here only when a decision affects the wider setting.

## Canon rules
- A brainstorm is not canon merely because it appears in a note.
- If two sources conflict, flag the conflict; do not quietly choose one.
- Distinguish objective setting facts from beliefs, propaganda, legends, and unreliable accounts.
- When changing established canon, record the old understanding, new decision, reason, and affected files.

## Open questions
| ID | Question | Why it matters | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-001 | What are the actual rules, costs, and limits of magic in the protagonist's original era and in the past? | Defines what the protagonist can plausibly reproduce or improve. | Establish the core magic system; decide which advances are practical engineering versus new theoretical discoveries. |
| Q-002 | What caused the protagonist's original death, and was the time displacement accidental or deliberate? | May define the central mystery and long-term plot. | Decide whether this is an early reveal, a mystery, or background. |
| Q-003 | What precisely motivated the duke's barony grant, and who understands it as exile? | Shapes family politics and the protagonist's starting position. | Define the duke's political circumstances. |
| Q-004 | What is the new continent like, and who already lives there? | Determines the frontier's opportunities, conflicts, and moral stakes. | Develop geography, inhabitants, and imperial claims without assuming the continent is empty. |
| Q-005 | What does the host's family, household, and local community expect from him? | Establishes immediate social pressure and practical obligations. | Build the host character and the barony's key relationships. |
| Q-006 | What tone and central conflict should guide the novel? | Helps focus worldbuilding on the story rather than building an encyclopedia. | Decide after clarifying the protagonist's goals. |
| Q-007 | What does magical industrialization actually consist of in the protagonist's era, and what stage has the earlier era reached? | Makes the technological gap concrete and determines which advances he can introduce. | Define a few representative differences in energy, production, transport, communication, tools, and institutions without committing to all of them at once. |
